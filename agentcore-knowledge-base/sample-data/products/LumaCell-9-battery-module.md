# Product: LumaCell-9 Lithium Battery Module

**SKU:** LC-9
**Category:** Energy Storage
**List price:** USD 640.00 per module
**Availability:** In stock (ships in 5 business days)

## Overview

The LumaCell-9 is a modular lithium-iron-phosphate (LiFePO4) battery pack for
backup power and off-grid energy storage. Modules can be stacked in series and
parallel to build larger systems.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Chemistry | LiFePO4 |
| Nominal voltage | 51.2 V |
| Capacity | 5.12 kWh per module |
| Cycle life | 6,000 cycles at 80% DoD |
| Communication | CAN bus, RS485 |
| Operating temperature | -10 °C to +50 °C |
| Warranty | 10 years |
